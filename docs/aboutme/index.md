## About Me- Bruno Salvi

My name is Bruno Salvi. I am a third-year mechanical engineering major at UNC Charlotte. As a student engineer, my progress towards my career is driven by my passion for the design process. I find the engineering process of analyzing, deciding, and communicating extremely rewarding, maintaining effort through trial and error to reach my goals. Focusing on communication, I enjoy the team effort involved with many engineering projects. Utilizing team members' unique strengths to create the most effective design process is something I find extremely satisfying.

When choosing an engineering major, I was heavily driven towards mechanical engineering. This is in large part due to the physical aspect of this branch of engineering that allows me to visualize and find satisfaction in completing projects with a physical, functioning product. Mechanical engineering also aligned with my career goals. Since I was younger, I had a very strong interest in the automotive and motorsports industries and was set on making a career out of this interest. I always found beauty in the functional design of cars and was interested in the decisions that led to their design, making mechanical engineering a natural choice to study.

As I grow closer to graduation, I find myself becoming an engineer who is passionate about the learning process and eager to continue improving my knowledge base. Through my experience, I have been able to narrow down my career interests into vehicle dynamics with applications in motorsports. I have also grown my ability to lead through projects and positions that emphasize leadership. These roles have shown me that I enjoy leading projects, as it allows me to get the best out of my projects and create extremely valuable connections with others. In the future, I hope to have more opportunities to learn skills that can help me become the best engineer I can be and work in an industry I have a deep passion for.
